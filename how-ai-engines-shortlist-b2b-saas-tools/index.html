How AI engines shortlist B2B SaaS tools

A citation study across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews: who gets named, which sources the engines lean on, and how rarely those shortlists agree. This is a meta-analysis of published research, not a fabricated prompt run.

In June 2025, Patrick Stox at Ahrefs compared the 50 most-mentioned sources in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews. The dataset covered about 76.7 million AI Overviews, 957,000 ChatGPT prompts and 953,500 Perplexity prompts in a single month. Only seven websites appeared in all three top-50 lists. Eighty-six percent of those leading sources were not shared.

For a B2B SaaS company, that split is no longer a curiosity about “AI SEO.” 6sense’s 2025 Buyer Experience Report, written up in November 2025 from nearly 4,000 buyers, found that 94% of B2B buyers now use a large language model during a purchase — up from the 89% Forrester recorded in mid-2024, the same step-up Vibin Babuurajan cited in a November 2026 interview. Those buyers are not asking the model to replace a vendor conversation. They are using it, 6sense found, mainly in the middle of the journey: to compare, synthesise and assemble a shortlist.

00

A short glossary, before the lists

The studies below use the same everyday words for slightly different things. The distinctions matter once you start comparing percentages.

Citation
A visible source link or named reference inside an AI answer. Profound and Semrush count citation events; Ahrefs Brand Radar also tracks mention share, which is not the same cut.
Mention, or brand mention
The brand is named in the answer, whether or not a URL is attached. SparkToro’s visibility percentage is a mention measure: how often a brand appears across many runs of a prompt.
Shortlist
The two, three or five tools an engine names when a buyer asks for “the best” in a category. 6sense’s buyers already knew most of the vendors they evaluated; the model is often confirming a set, not inventing one.
AI search visibility, for SaaS
How consistently a product is named or cited when a buying-intent prompt is run across engines and over repeat runs — not a single screenshot, and not a “ranking position” in the chat.
GEO, or generative engine optimisation
The practice of earning those mentions and citations. Epic Slope’s FAQ puts it as work that starts out looking like SEO and eventually becomes brand building, the same line Vibin used in the MediaNews4U interview.
AI Overviews and AI Mode
Google’s generated answers on a search results page (Overviews) and the more conversational, sidebar-heavy AI Mode. Semrush’s July 2025 study treats them as related but not identical systems. They are not ChatGPT.
Chat assistants: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot
Answer engines that retrieve, generate, and — when browsing is on — cite. Default ChatGPT, as Ahrefs noted, may not cite at all. Gemini appears in several Ahrefs datasets but has fewer public source-share tables than the other three.
Owned, earned, community, review, reference
A rough taxonomy of citation sources used on this page: the vendor’s own site; editorial and news; Reddit, Quora, YouTube comments; G2, Capterra, Gartner; and Wikipedia or similar references.
01

Only seven sources sit on every engine’s shortlist

The surprising number is not that Wikipedia is cited a lot. It is that the lists of leading sources barely overlap.

Stox’s June 2025 note is short and blunt. Of the 50 most-mentioned websites in Ahrefs Brand Radar for AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity, only seven were in the top 50 for all three. That is 14% shared, 86% not. The accompanying essay, “The 10 most mentioned domains,” uses the same June 2025 window and puts the combined volume at about 78.6 million searches.

The biases he recorded still explain a lot of what other firms measured later. AI Overviews, he wrote, lean toward the authoritative sites already familiar from search — health, finance, encyclopedic knowledge, social media — and toward Google-owned properties, with media, non-Google entertainment and ecommerce notably thinner. ChatGPT leans on publishers and non-Google media, and has licensing deals with many of the sites it cites; it is thin on health. Perplexity pulls from a broader international corpus, with local and regional brands that never made the other two top-50 lists, and with less of the usual Western media and social web.

Vibin Babuurajan, November 2026

Vibin used a simple mental model in that MediaNews4U interview: SEO asks whether you can rank a page; AI search asks whether the wider information ecosystem considers you a credible answer. The Ahrefs overlap is the same idea in a Venn diagram. A page-one ranking on Google is one ecosystem. It is not, by itself, the ecosystem ChatGPT or Perplexity will consult.

02

Each engine has its own set of habits

Web pages, AI answers, videos and review sites do not travel through one machine. Filter the cards for a single engine, or leave them on “all four.”

ChatGPT

Licensed publishers, then a long tail

Profound, across 680 million citations from August 2024 to June 2025 (updated August 2025), put Wikipedia at 7.8% of ChatGPT’s total citations, well ahead of Reddit at 1.8%, then Forbes and G2 at 1.1% each. Among ChatGPT’s own top ten sources, Wikipedia alone was 47.9% of that top-ten share. Ahrefs, on a June 2025 mention-share cut, put Wikipedia higher still, at 16.3%.

  • Freshness: the strongest preference in Ahrefs’ July 2025 study of 17 million citations. ChatGPT in-text citations averaged 958 days since publication, against 1,416 for organic SERPs.
  • Google overlap: about 8% of in-text citation URLs also sat in Google’s top 10 for the same prompt (Ahrefs, August 2025, 15,000 prompts).
  • Volatility: Semrush watched ChatGPT’s Reddit share fall from close to 60% of prompt responses in early August 2025 to around 10% by mid-September, and Wikipedia from about 55% to under 20% — and both remained ChatGPT’s two most-cited domains after the drop.
Perplexity

Live search, closer to the SERP, heavy on Reddit

Profound’s overall cut for the same August 2024–June 2025 window put Reddit at 6.6% of Perplexity citations, YouTube at 2.0%, Gartner at 1.0%, and G2 at 0.6%. Among Perplexity’s top ten, Reddit was 46.7% of that share. Ahrefs’ June 2025 mention-share figure for Wikipedia on Perplexity was 12.5%.

  • It is the assistant that aligns closest with Google in Ahrefs’ URL-level study: 28.6% of cited URLs were also in Google’s top 10, against about 8% for the other assistants.
  • Semrush’s July 2025 AI Mode study, using a domain-level cut rather than URLs, found a much higher 91% domain overlap and 82% URL overlap with Google’s top 10. The two figures are not interchangeable; they are both reported in the appendix.
  • Its top sources in Semrush’s later three-month study (230,000 prompts, more than 100 million citations, into October 2025) included Reddit, LinkedIn, NIH, Microsoft and Google.
Gemini

Present in the datasets, thinner in the source tables

Gemini is in Ahrefs’ August 2025 overlap study and the July 2025 freshness study, and it is one of the five platforms in Epic Slope’s published ZEPIC results. Public, dated source-share tables as detailed as Profound’s ChatGPT–Perplexity–Overviews cuts were not in the studies we reviewed. That is a gap, not a zero.

  • Overlap with Google’s top 10, for Gemini as for Copilot, sits in the “hovering around 8%” group Ahrefs described; the paper does not print a standalone Gemini percentage in the text we captured.
  • Freshness: Gemini citations averaged 1,118 days since publication and 831 days since the last update — newer than organic results (1,416 and 1,047 days), less aggressive than ChatGPT.
  • ZEPIC, on Ahrefs Brand Radar from February 2025 to March 2026, was cited four times in Gemini, against 26 times in ChatGPT and ten in AI Overviews. That is one product’s record, not a category law.
Google AI Overviews

From the index, then a widening gap with page one

Profound put Reddit at 2.2% of AI Overview citations, YouTube at 1.9%, Quora at 1.5% and LinkedIn at 1.3%, with Wikipedia only 0.6% — a very different shape from ChatGPT. Ahrefs’ June 2025 mention share still had Wikipedia first on Overviews, at 8.4%, with Reddit and Quora high only there (7.4% and 3.6%). YouTube, Ahrefs later wrote, became the most-cited domain in Overviews and grew 34% over six months.

  • In July 2025 Ahrefs found 76% of Overview citation URLs also sat in Google’s top 10. By the 2 March 2026 update, on 863,000 keyword SERPs and 4 million Overview URLs, that figure was 37.9% in the article's prose. The table in the same piece prints a slightly different split — 37.10% in the top 10, 26.20% in positions 11–100, and 36.70% outside the top 100 — and we do not choose between them.
  • Of the citations that did not rank in the top 100, 18.2% were YouTube URLs, or 5.6% of all Overview URLs in that dataset.
  • Semrush, on more than 10 million keywords, saw Overviews trigger on 6.49% of queries in January 2025, peak at 24.61% in July, and settle at 15.69% in November. Commercial intent’s share of those queries rose from 8.15% to 18.57% by October. Computers & electronics, a neighbour of SaaS, had Overviews on 17.92% of keywords in November, second only to science.

Profound’s percentages are shares of all citations in a long-tail distribution, so even a “leading” source is often in the low single digits. Ahrefs’ mention-share figures are a different cut, on a different month. Both are dated in the appendix. Semrush’s AI Mode, which is not the same product as AI Overviews, cited an average of seven unique domains per query, against three for Overviews, and put Reddit, YouTube and Facebook in 68% or more of the answers that carried extra links.

03

A worked example: a mid-market CRM prompt, and a SaaS cloud shortlist

Resoneo’s ranking-signals essay walks through a real search, “[knee pain when running],” captured on a dated day, in two countries. We tried the equivalent for a buying-intent SaaS prompt. The live four-engine capture did not come back. The published record for this kind of prompt is still specific, and it is dated.

Buying-intent prompt · attempted 13 November 2026, 04:50–05:00 IST What are the best CRM tools for mid-market B2B SaaS companies?

We fetched Google Search for the US and Perplexity’s public search URL through Firecrawl. Google’s automated response did not include an AI Overview. Perplexity returned a signed-out shell — “Sign up and repeat your request” — and no shortlist. ChatGPT and Gemini, on the public web, also required a session. No vendor names from that attempt are reported here, because none were collected.

What 6sense says this prompt is for

In the 2025 Buyer Experience Report, buyers used LLMs mainly in the middle of the journey, not at the start or the close. Eighty-five percent already had direct prior experience with the vendors they evaluated. Example queries 6sense itself lists include “What are the key differences between Salesforce and HubSpot for mid-market B2B companies?” and “Create a comparison table of marketing automation platforms.” A CRM shortlist prompt sits in that band: comparison and synthesis, before a sales call, not instead of one.

6sense / Dan Hieb, 24 November 2025, from nearly 4,000 buyers. Median purchase $300,000–$400,000. Average buying group 10.1 people. Vendors evaluated: 5.1, of which 3.9 were already known.
What Seer says Google will often wrap around it

Seer Interactive’s April 2026 AIO CTR update, on 5.47 million tracked queries and 2.43 billion organic impressions, found that comparison queries triggered an AI Overview 95% of the time, and question-format queries 86% of the time. A prompt of this shape, if it is also typed into Google, is in the highest-exposure band of that study. Being cited inside that Overview, Seer found, delivered 120% more organic clicks per impression than sitting on the same SERP uncited — and still 38% fewer clicks than a query with no Overview at all.

Seer Interactive, “AIO Impact on Google CTR: 2026 Update,” 24 April 2026. Data from January 2025 to February 2026, 53 brands. Informational math per 1 million impressions: no AIO ≈ 33,500 clicks; brand cited ≈ 20,743; AIO present, not cited ≈ 9,445.

The closest dated SaaS shortlist we can actually cite

Rand Fishkin and Patrick O’Donnell at SparkToro and Gumshoe, in research published in January 2026, asked 600 volunteers to run 12 prompts through ChatGPT, Claude and Google’s AI Overview (or AI Mode if an Overview did not show), a combined 2,961 times, over November and December 2025. One of those topic spaces was cloud-computing providers for SaaS startups.

They did not print a ranked “top five clouds” as a single canonical list — and that is the finding. Even in a tight category, with relatively few plausible vendors and a higher pairwise correlation than a sprawling topic such as new science-fiction novels, the tools almost never returned the same list in the same order: fewer than one time in 1,000, “no matter the question,” including “SaaS Cloud Computing Providers.” Across the whole experiment, an identical brand list appeared fewer than once in 100 runs. Visibility percentage across many runs, Fishkin concluded, is a reasonable metric. A “ranking position in AI” is not.

The same idea, from the other side of the table

Vibin used a client story in the November 2026 interview: the CEO of an enterprise security company came close to losing a deal because a buyer searched the category in AI search and did not see them, while several competitors appeared. They closed it another way. He also used a blunt frequency example: the same question, asked by two people, can produce different answers, and a brand might be mentioned in 20 searches out of 100. “Ideally, you want that number to be as close to 100% as possible, but there’s no guarantee.” SparkToro’s <1-in-100 identical lists is the measurement behind that sentence.

SparkToro publication: January 2026 (article assets dated January 2026; fieldwork November–December 2025). Claude was in SparkToro’s three-tool set; Gemini and Perplexity were not. We do not back-fill those two engines with invented SaaS-cloud names.

Two things in those bars should matter to a SaaS company more than the precise decimal. First, G2 already appears as a measurable ChatGPT and Perplexity source in a general web crawl — 1.1% and 0.6% of all citations, in a dataset where almost every other software vendor’s own domain would be a rounding error. Gartner shows up on Perplexity and on Overviews. The review layer is not a side channel. Second, the community and video layer — Reddit, YouTube, Quora, LinkedIn — is not optional on Overviews or Perplexity, and it is not stable.

Luke Harsel’s Semrush study, published 10 November 2025, watched that instability in public. Over three months, on 230,000 prompts and more than 100 million citations across ChatGPT search, Google AI Mode and Perplexity, ChatGPT’s Reddit and Wikipedia citations collapsed in mid-September 2025 and then restabilised at a lower, more balanced ratio. Reddit and Wikipedia were still ChatGPT’s two most-cited domains in October. LinkedIn and Forbes rose on the same timeline. The drop was not mirrored on AI Mode or Perplexity. Citations, Semrush’s title slide might as well have said, are not a setting you configure once.

What Vibin used to tell companies to do with this map. In the same interview he used a four-point list: make the content findable by AI agents; write around the categories you want to be associated with; build visibility beyond the website, through third-party publications; and be present on the channels the engines actually cite — “whether that’s Reddit, YouTube, LinkedIn, or third-party review platforms like G2 and Capterra.” That list is almost a caption for the Profound bars. He also used a warning that sits with the review-site numbers: if a financial-planning product’s site talks about simplicity and Reddit or G2 says the opposite, “there’s very little room to fake it.”

Ahrefs’ June 2025 mention share, the other cut

On Brand Radar’s mention-share measure, Wikipedia still dominated all three of ChatGPT (16.3%), Perplexity (12.5%) and AI Overviews (8.4%). Reddit and Quora, Stox wrote, “rank high only in AI Overviews (7.4% and 3.6%). They don’t crack the top-10 in ChatGPT or Perplexity.” That last sentence is from June 2025. Semrush’s August–September ChatGPT figures, and Profound’s year-long ChatGPT Reddit share of 1.8%, show a different window and a different ChatGPT. The honest way to read them together is not to pick a favourite percentage. It is to treat source share as dated and engine-specific, and to re-measure.

05

Consistency is the signal, not a single mention

If an engine almost never returns the same shortlist twice, a screenshot is a poor KPI. The studies converge on a different one: how often the brand appears, across prompts, engines and independent sources.

Fishkin’s January 2026 conclusions are the cleanest statement of that. The tools are probability engines, “designed to generate unique answers every time.” Users almost never craft similar prompts even when they share an intent, so the wild variation is likely higher than his controlled experiment showed. With enough prompts, run enough times, a visibility percentage becomes a dartboard you can actually read. A tool that sells a ranking position in the answer, he wrote, is “full of baloney.”

Vibin used the same test, in different words, when he was asked how you know a citation reflects quality rather than a quirk of training data. A single citation, he said, could be a quirk. “The stronger signal is consistency. If a company keeps showing up across different AI engines, different prompts, and multiple independent sources — whether that’s comparison sites, reviews, communities, or industry publications — you’re seeing something much closer to genuine authority.” The question he used was not “Did ChatGPT mention us?” It was: “Are we consistently being mentioned and cited when our category is discussed, across different questions, engines, and independent sources?”

What to measure

Visibility percentage across a fixed prompt set, re-run on a schedule, on each engine separately — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, AI Overviews — and across the set. Report the range, not a point. Note the date and the model.

What not to treat as a rank

The order of names in one answer. SparkToro found the same ordered list fewer than once in 1,000 runs, even in tight SaaS-cloud space. Semrush showed that a source’s share can move tens of points in a month without a corresponding change on another engine.

06

Rankings still help, less than they did, and more at the domain than the URL

A page-one ranking is neither necessary nor sufficient for a citation. It is also not irrelevant — especially at the domain level, and especially for Google’s own AI surfaces, which have been loosening their grip on the top 10.

The March 2026 Overview update used 863,000 keyword SERPs and 4 million Overview URLs, more than double the earlier analysis. The same article's prose and its table disagree on how the remaining Overview citations split between positions 11–100 and beyond the top 100, so those remainder percentages are not used as headline figures here. Eighty percent of the assistant citations in the August study, Ahrefs wrote, did not rank in Google at all for the original prompt.

Semrush, in its 21 July 2025 AI Mode study, reported much higher overlap at the domain level: about 86% domain and 67% URL overlap for AI Overviews with Google’s top 10, and more than 91% domain and 82% URL overlap for Perplexity. Those are not a rebuttal of Ahrefs’ 12% and 28.6%. They are a different grain (domain versus URL), a different period, and a different sample. Semrush’s own later comment is the useful one: domains that accumulate many top-10 rankings are far more likely to be cited, even when the cited URL is not the page-one URL. In a related Semrush study, most of ChatGPT’s citations in that sample came from URLs ranking at position 21 or beyond.

YouTube mentions, 0.737

Louise Linehan’s 12 December 2025 Ahrefs study of 75,000 brands (domains with DR above 40 and a head keyword of at least 800 monthly searches) found that YouTube mentions — a brand name in a video title, transcript or description — had the strongest correlation with AI visibility, about 0.737, ahead of YouTube mention impressions at about 0.717 and branded web mentions at 0.66–0.71. The pattern held on ChatGPT as well as on Google’s own surfaces. OpenAI’s GPT-4, the New York Times had reported in 2024, was trained on more than a million hours of YouTube transcriptions. Correlation is not causation; Ahrefs said so plainly.

Domain rating, 0.266, on ChatGPT

The same study found ChatGPT correlated only weakly with classic authority metrics: branded search volume at 0.352 and domain rating at 0.266. The number of site pages correlated at about 0.194 — “almost no relationship,” in Linehan’s words. Ryan Law’s line, quoted there, was that this is “not just a content creation arms race.” AI Mode, by contrast, showed the highest correlations with branded anchors (0.628) and branded search volume (0.466). The three assistants largely mentioned the same brands, with an output-overlap correlation of 0.779 — which is about brands, not about the URLs they cite.

Freshness cuts the same way, and not evenly. Ryan Law’s 28 July 2025 study of 16.975 million cited URLs found AI-cited pages 25.7% “fresher” than organic results on publication age (1,064 days versus 1,432), and 13.1% fresher on last update. ChatGPT had the strongest preference for new publication; Google AI Overviews, at 1,432 days, looked almost like the organic SERP, and in the table were slightly older than it. Perplexity appeared to order its citations from newer to older. A SaaS changelog, a comparison page that still says “2024,” and a G2 profile last refreshed this quarter are not equivalent inputs.

07

Two SaaS companies that moved the number

The figures in this section are Epic Slope’s published case results, attributed to the firm’s own case studies. They are not measurements we ran for this meta-analysis. They are here because they show what “consistency across engines” looked like for two B2B products once the work was treated as one programme.

Composio · December 2025 – June 2026

From fewer than 10 citations to about 180

Composio is a developer-first integration platform. Over a six-month engagement covering SEO, GEO, programmatic SEO and link building, Epic Slope reported that tracked AI citations grew from fewer than 10 to roughly 180 across major LLM platforms — about 18 times — with “447.1K total AI citations and 240 average cited pages over the period.” Visibility across core bottom-of-funnel themes rose from under 7% to 24%.

Organic clicks, in the same write-up, went from 250,000 to 754,000 against the previous six months; impressions from 12.4 million to 55.9 million; average position from 9.2 to 7.2. Domain rating moved from 64 to 74. Clicks on commercial searches went from 1,800 to 3,940, with average position from 16.7 to 7.9, and conversions from organic search roughly tripled. The GEO work, the case study says, focused on making content easier for AI systems to understand and cite, “with a focus on the bottom-of-funnel topics that shape tool shortlists.”

Source: Epic Slope Partners, “How We Turned Composio’s Organic & AI Search Into a Lead-Gen Engine,” attributed to work with Sunil Kumar Dash, Content and Growth, Composio. The “447.1K total AI citations” line is quoted as published; it sits beside the “roughly 180” tracked-citation figure and should be read as a separate Brand Radar-style total, not as 180 × something we were not given.

ZEPIC · 12 months, citations compared February 2025 – March 2026

42 citations, split across five platforms

ZEPIC is a marketing OS. In 12 months, Epic Slope reported, domain rating grew from 20 to 55, organic traffic increased more than ten times, 11 keywords reached the top three, and ZEPIC was cited 42 times across five AI platforms. Ahrefs Brand Radar, comparing February 2025 with March 2026, showed that split as 26 citations in ChatGPT, 10 in Google AI Overviews, 4 in Gemini, “along with references in Perplexity and Copilot.” Monthly traffic value, the case study says, grew to $178 from less than $10 at the start.

The work described is the same shape as the source map in section 04: placements in product ecosystems and on high-DR editorial domains; pages across the journey, including comparisons and alternatives; and pages reformatted with direct answers and clear definitions “so that both search engines and AI systems” could reuse them. Referring domains expanded more than six-fold.

Source: Epic Slope Partners, “How ZEPIC Built AI Visibility & 10x Organic Traffic,” attributed to work with Sreelesh Pillai, co-CEO at ZEPIC. The 26 + 10 + 4 already sum to 40; the remaining two sit in the Perplexity and Copilot remainder as published, without a further split.

How the founders used to describe the same work

When Vinayak Ravi, Vibin Babuurajan and Gautham Ramakrishnan started Epic Slope Partners in January 2024, Vibin later said, they used that first year to do the work themselves — selling, managing clients, wearing every hat. The three, he said in a LinkedIn post, used to sit on the client side of SaaS companies; the firm’s own site presents that experience as more than 42 years of cumulative demand generation, “from $0 to $400M,” with logos for Freshworks, IBM, CleverTap, Zoho, Adpushup, Locad, Turing and Sprinklr. A MediaNews4U interviewer put the span as IBM, Freshworks, Zoho, Sprinklr and CleverTap. We do not assign those employers to one founder except where a public source is specific: Vibin used to work in growth at Freshworks, and he later used “learnings from my time at Freshworks and Turing” in a LinkedIn post of his own. Gautham’s public profile centres on paid-media ROI across Google, LinkedIn and Meta; Deepakshi Dawar, then head of marketing at Xflow, wrote that Gautham and Vinayak used a data-backed plan on organic growth. Vibin used to describe GEO as work that “starts by looking like SEO, but eventually… starts looking a lot more like brand building.” The YouTube and branded-mention correlations in section 06 are what that sentence looks like as a coefficient.

08

Takeaways, for a SaaS team that has to choose what to do on Monday

  1. Measure visibility percentage, on a fixed prompt set, across engines, and date it. SparkToro’s experiment is the reason. A single ChatGPT screenshot is an anecdote. A “position in the answer” is not a metric the tools themselves support. Re-run the same buying-intent prompts on ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and AI Overviews, often enough to get a percentage, and keep the model and the week in the spreadsheet.
  2. Do not assume a Google top-10 URL will be the cited URL, especially outside Google’s own surfaces. Ahrefs’ 12% average for ChatGPT, Gemini and Copilot, and the fall in Overview-to-top-10 overlap from 76% to 37.9%, are the current published record. Domain-level authority, Semrush found, still travels; the exact URL often does not.
  3. Build for the sources each engine actually cites, not for a generic “AI list.” ChatGPT, in Profound’s year-long cut, leaned on Wikipedia, then Reddit, Forbes and G2. Perplexity leaned on Reddit, YouTube and Gartner. Overviews leaned on Reddit, YouTube, Quora and LinkedIn. Eighty-six percent of leading sources are not shared. A Reddit-only plan and a Wikipedia-only plan each miss two engines.
  4. Treat G2, Gartner, Capterra, Reddit, YouTube and LinkedIn as part of the product’s public evidence, not as a comms afterthought. That is both the citation data and the line Vibin used. It is also the honesty constraint he used: a site that claims simplicity will not get the citation it wants if G2 and Reddit say otherwise.
  5. Prefer brand mentions, YouTube mentions and third-party agreement over a raw increase in page count. Ahrefs’ 75,000-brand study found almost no relationship between the number of site pages and AI visibility, and only a weak ChatGPT correlation with domain rating. YouTube mentions and branded web mentions were the strong correlates. Programmatic SEO can still expand the index of citable URLs — Composio’s case study credits it for impression growth — but it is not, on this evidence, a substitute for being talked about.
  6. Re-measure after every engine change. Semrush watched ChatGPT’s Reddit share fall from about 60% of responses to about 10% in a few weeks of September 2025, without a matching collapse on Perplexity or AI Mode. Source maps expire.
  7. On Google, a citation is the better of two bad CTRs, and comparison queries are the exposed ones. Seer’s 2026 update: +120% clicks versus uncited on the same Overview SERP, still −38% versus a query with no Overview; comparison queries trigger an Overview 95% of the time. SaaS “alternatives” and “versus” pages live in that 95%.
  8. GEO, on this evidence, does start as SEO and become brand building. That is the sentence Vibin used. The studies above are what it rests on: retrieval systems that look past page one, source lists that privilege third-party evidence, and a consistency test no single article can pass on its own.

Sources and method

This is a meta-analysis. It does not contain a new, complete four-engine prompt panel run by us. Every quantitative claim is attributed to a published study, a dated interview, or an Epic Slope case study, and is collected in the inventory below.

What we did

  • On 12–13 November 2026 we scraped the primary studies with Firecrawl, and read them in full rather than from secondary round-ups. Where a later update superseded an earlier figure (Ahrefs’ Overview-to-top-10 overlap; Semrush’s refreshed Overviews study), we report both and date both.
  • We attempted a live capture of the prompt “What are the best CRM tools for mid-market B2B SaaS companies?” on 13 November 2026. Google Search, fetched automatically for hl=en&gl=us, did not return an AI Overview in that fetch. Perplexity’s public search URL returned a signed-out gate. ChatGPT and Gemini were not available unsigned. We report that failure and do not invent a shortlist.
  • Founder background is limited to facts we could source: Epic Slope’s FAQ and homepage, Vibin’s 2 November 2026 MediaNews4U interview, his LinkedIn posts, a YouTube episode description and LinkedIn commentary naming him as an ex-Freshworks growth leader, Gautham’s public LinkedIn headline, and a named Xflow testimonial. We do not assign IBM, Zoho, Sprinklr or CleverTap to a single founder. We did not obtain a full signed-out scrape of the three LinkedIn profile pages, so we do not quote education or role histories from those pages.
  • Epic Slope’s Composio and ZEPIC figures are quoted from the firm’s published case studies and labelled as such. Client numbers that do not appear there are not used.

What we did not do

  • We did not use DataForSEO, or any paid rank-tracking panel, for this page.
  • We did not merge incompatible percentages (Ahrefs URL overlap, Semrush domain overlap, Profound total-citation share, Ahrefs mention share) into a single “true” share. Where they disagree, both appear, with their grain and their dates.
  • We did not have a Gemini-specific top-source table of the same depth as Profound’s ChatGPT, Perplexity and Overviews cuts, and we say so.
  • We did not transcribe names from chart images where the surrounding article did not also print them as text — including the seven websites that sit in all three Ahrefs top-50 lists.
A note on conflicting overlap figures. Ahrefs, August 2025: 28.6% of Perplexity citation URLs were in Google’s top 10, and about 8% for ChatGPT, Gemini and Copilot, averaging 12%. Semrush, July 2025: Perplexity had more than 91% domain overlap and 82% URL overlap with Google’s top 10; AI Overviews about 86% domain and 67% URL. These can both be right in their own samples. They should not be averaged.
