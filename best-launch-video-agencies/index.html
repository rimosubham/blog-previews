Best Launch Video Agencies for Startups (2026 Buyer's Guide)

Ten launch video agencies compared on who they suit, what they charge, how fast they ship, and whether they help get the video seen.

What is a launch video agency (and how is it different from a video production company)?

A launch video agency makes the short video a startup posts when it announces something. The good ones also help plan the launch day around it. A general video production company makes the film and hands it over. A launch video agency is judged on what happens in the first few hours after posting.

That difference matters more than most founders expect. A polished film posted with no distribution plan often gets a few thousand views from your own followers and then disappears. On X and LinkedIn, the first hour decides how far the algorithm pushes a post. So a launch partner usually has to cover four things:

  1. Angle and script: the first line and the first three seconds.
  2. Production: recording the founder, editing, motion design, and cutdowns for each platform.
  3. Launch-day timing: picking the day and hour, and lining it up with press or a funding announcement.
  4. Amplification: founders, operators, investors, and creators who quote, repost, and reply during the launch window.

Most agencies on this list are strong at steps 1–2. Only a few own steps 3–4. That's the main split to understand before you compare prices.

Video itself isn't optional any more. In Wyzowl's 2026 survey, 91% of businesses use video as a marketing tool. Only 10% of video marketers rely entirely on outside vendors. The rest make video in-house or mix in-house work with vendors. For a launch, where there's one chance to get it right, an outside specialist is often the better bet.

How to choose a launch video agency

1. Decide what the video is for. Is it there to win launch day (views, signups, demos, pre-orders in 24–48 hours), or to be an evergreen homepage asset? Launch day points to a distribution-led specialist. An evergreen asset points to a production studio. Many startups need both, but one usually matters more.

2. Ask who runs launch day. Ask every agency: "Who posts, when, and who amplifies it in the first hour?" If the answer is "you do", budget your own time, or a separate amplification partner.

3. Look at proof you can click. Ask for links to live posts, not showreels. Check the replies and quote posts as well as the view count. A post with millions of views and very few likes or replies deserves a follow-up question.

4. Match the format to the product. Founder-led, on-camera videos build trust for fundraises and new categories. Motion design works for UI-heavy software. Narrative or comedic films work for consumer and brand moments. Our breakdown of the tech launch video formats that actually work goes through 20 of them.

5. Fit the timeline to your launch date. If you launch in 10 days, a 4–6 week studio is the wrong choice, however good it is.

6. Read the contract for ownership and cutdowns. Get the raw footage, the platform cutdowns (vertical, square, 6s and 15s) and the rights in writing.

How much does a startup launch video cost in 2026?

Based on what agencies publish, a startup launch video costs about $5,000–$25,000 from launch specialists. Premium studios and broader productions can cost a lot more. Here's how the published prices line up:

Under $5k Animated or explainer-style video, or you supply the footage What a Story (from $3,500), Unveil Launch Pro ($5k), Flowjam animated ($5k)
$5k–$15k Filmed founder-led launch film, a single location, fast edit Unveil Cinematic ($8k) and Narrative ($12k)
$15k–$25k Launch film plus some launch-day distribution Flowjam $15k (100k-view floor) and $25k (500k-view floor)
$25k+ Full crew, multiple locations, PR and amplification options Clickstrike (from $25k)
Quote-based View-guaranteed launches or premium brand films The Launch Video Company (1M/3M/5M tiers), Sandwich, Vidico, Represent, Shown Media

For a wider benchmark, Vidico's 2026 guide gives a range of $10,000–$150,000+ for a product launch video, and $3,000–$25,000 for an animated explainer.

What moves the price: shoot days and locations, travel, actors, animation complexity, the number of platform cutdowns, revision rounds, and above all whether distribution is included. A $5k video with no audience and a $25k launch with a guaranteed view floor are different products. Compare them on cost per outcome, not cost per minute of footage.

How long does it take to make a launch video?

Published timelines range from 5 business days to 6 weeks:

  • 5–9 business days from the shoot (Unveil).
  • 10 days for an animated launch video (Flowjam).
  • 2–3 weeks for end-to-end production with multiple variants (The Launch Video Company's launch video playbook). Compressed launches are possible. The HydraDB launch was five days, and Mave Health went from Friday to Wednesday on the distribution side.
  • 4–6 weeks from kickoff (Clickstrike).

The bottleneck is usually not editing. It's script approval and founder availability. Lock the angle early and book the shoot day before anything else.

YC's advice is also worth keeping in mind: launching isn't one moment. Its talk How to launch (again and again) encourages startups to launch over and over to different audiences. Plan your launch video so it can be re-cut for the next launch too.

Launch video agency checklist

Before you sign, make sure you can tick these off:

  • ☐ A one-line goal for the launch (for example "1,000 waitlist signups in 48 hours"), not just "go viral".
  • ☐ Links to 3+ live launch posts from the agency, with engagement you can check.
  • ☐ A clear answer on who handles launch-day distribution, and whether it costs extra.
  • ☐ A fixed launch date, with script, shoot and review deadlines worked back from it.
  • ☐ Deliverables listed: hero cut, vertical and square versions, short cutdowns, captions (most feeds autoplay on mute), thumbnail.
  • ☐ Platform specs confirmed (for example LinkedIn's video ad specifications if you'll boost the post).
  • ☐ Ownership: the footage and final files are assigned to you in writing. Under U.S. law, a commissioned work only counts as "made for hire" in certain categories and with a written agreement, so put it in the contract.
  • ☐ A revision policy, plus what happens if the result misses the agreed bar (refund, make-good or re-cut).

The best launch video agency is the one whose strengths match your launch-day goal. If your launch has to be seen, start with the agencies that own distribution. If it has to last, start with the studios known for craft. Then compare published prices and timelines against your launch date.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best launch video agency for startups?+

It depends on what you need from launch day. If you want views and pipeline on X and LinkedIn, pick a distribution-led specialist such as The Launch Video Company, Shown Media, or Flowjam's distribution tiers. If you need a fast, fairly priced film for a YC-style launch, Unveil or Flowjam fit. If you want a signature brand film, look at Sandwich, Vidico or Represent Studio.

How much does a startup launch video cost?+

Published prices for startup launch videos run from about $3,500–$5,000 for animated or self-shot videos to $25,000+ for full-crew productions. Packages that include guaranteed distribution are often quoted on a call. Vidico's industry benchmark for product launch videos is $10,000–$150,000+.

How long does it take to produce a launch video?+

Between 5 business days and 6 weeks. Fast studios deliver in 5–10 days. End-to-end production with distribution usually takes 2–3 weeks. Full-crew agencies quote 4–6 weeks.

Do launch video agencies guarantee views?+

Some do. The Launch Video Company sells 1M, 3M and 5M view tiers. Flowjam's $15k and $25k packages include 100,000 and 500,000 view minimums. Production-only studios don't guarantee views, because they don't run distribution.

Is a launch video agency worth it for an early-stage startup?+

It's usually worth it when the launch is tied to a fundraise, a big product release, or a deadline like YC Demo Day, where one strong day can drive signups, demos or pre-orders. For small feature updates, a founder-shot video posted with a good hook is often enough.

Should distribution be part of the launch video engagement?+

If launch-day reach matters, yes. A produced video with no distribution plan usually only reaches your existing followers. At the very least, agree who will amplify the post in the first hour.

How long should a startup launch video be?+

Most launch videos for X and LinkedIn run 30–90 seconds. The hook (problem or bold claim) comes in the first three seconds, and there's one clear call to action. Make shorter 6–15 second cutdowns for ads and reposts.

What's the difference between a launch video agency and a freelancer?+

A freelancer can edit or animate well and costs less. An agency covers strategy, scripting, production and often distribution, and is accountable for the whole launch. Managing several freelancers yourself takes a lot of founder time in launch week.

Who owns the launch video after the project?+

Whoever your contract says. Ask for a written assignment of copyright, or a work-made-for-hire clause where it applies, plus delivery of raw footage and project files.

Can I use an agency just for amplification if I made my own video?+

Yes. The Launch Video Company offers an amplification-only option. Edison Scientific and Mave Health both launched that way.
